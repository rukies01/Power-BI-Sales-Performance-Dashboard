# Sales Performance & Profitability Dashboard

### Power BI | DAX | Sales Analytics | KPI Reporting | Profitability Analysis

A Power BI capstone project analysing sales performance across products, sales
agents and stores, with a focus on revenue, profitability, transaction volume
and weekly performance against targets.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project was developed as a capstone project for the **Microsoft Power BI
Desktop for Business Intelligence** course on Udemy.

The objective was to transform sales data into an interactive business
intelligence dashboard that enables users to monitor overall performance,
investigate product profitability, compare sales-agent performance and track
weekly KPIs against business targets.

The dataset was provided as part of the course materials, while the Power BI dashboard, analytical implementation and RS visual branding were developed by me. The course author permits the project materials to be used publicly for portfolio purposes.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The dashboard was designed to answer questions such as:

- How much revenue and profit is the business generating?
- What is the overall cost of generating sales?
- How many transactions have been completed?
- Which product categories generate the most profit?
- Which sales agents generate the most revenue?
- How is sales performance changing over time?
- Are current-week revenue, profit, cost and transaction levels meeting their
  targets?
- Which individual products contribute most strongly to revenue and profit?
- How does profitability vary across products and stores?

---

## 📊 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard tracks core sales and profitability metrics including:

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Total Revenue | Measures overall sales generated |
| Total Profit | Measures profit generated from sales |
| Total Cost | Tracks the cost associated with sales |
| Total Transactions | Measures transaction volume |
| Profit Margin | Evaluates profitability relative to revenue |
| Current Week Revenue | Tracks weekly revenue performance |
| Current Week Profit | Tracks weekly profitability |
| Current Week Cost | Monitors weekly costs |
| Current Week Transactions | Tracks weekly transaction activity |

At the overall dashboard level, the analysis shows approximately:

- **$38M Total Revenue**
- **$11M Total Profit**
- **$27M Total Cost**
- **13.73K Total Transactions**

## 📈 Dashboard 1 — Executive Sales Performance

The executive dashboard provides a high-level view of overall business
performance.

It combines headline KPIs with trend and comparative analysis across products
and sales agents.

### Analysis includes:

- Revenue and profit trends over time
- Profit by product category
- Revenue by sales agent
- Current-week transaction performance
- Current-week revenue
- Current-week cost
- Current-week profit
- Performance against predefined targets
- Interactive filtering by sales agent

This page is designed to allow decision-makers to quickly understand overall
sales performance before investigating individual performance drivers.

---

## 🔍 Dashboard 2 — Product & Store Performance

The detailed analysis page provides a more granular view of sales performance.

Product-level metrics include:

- Total Transactions
- Total Revenue
- Total Profit
- Profit Margin
- Total Cost

Conditional formatting and data bars make it easier to compare products and
identify stronger and weaker performers.

The page also includes:

- Weekly filtering
- Product-level performance comparison
- Store-level revenue analysis
- Profitability comparison across products

This enables users to move from executive-level KPIs into more detailed
performance investigation.

## 💡 Analytical Observations

### Revenue and Profitability

The dashboard shows approximately **$38M in total revenue** and **$11M in
profit**, with total costs of approximately **$27M**.

This indicates that analysing revenue alone would not provide a complete view
of business performance. Profit, cost and profit margin need to be considered
alongside sales volume.

### Product Performance

Profitability varies across product categories and individual products.

The detailed product view allows high-revenue products to be compared with
their profit margins, helping distinguish between products that generate large
sales volumes and those that generate stronger profitability.

### Sales Agent Performance

Revenue differs considerably across sales agents.

The sales-agent ranking enables the business to identify stronger performers
and investigate potential differences in customer portfolios, territories or
sales activity.

### Weekly Performance vs. Targets

The dashboard compares current-week KPIs with predefined performance goals.

This makes it possible to identify areas where performance is above or below
expectations and provides a starting point for further investigation.
